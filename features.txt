✅ 1. A Central Employee Database (Employee Master Record)

Every platform stores all employee information in one centralized system, including:

Personal details

Job details

Compensation

Documents/contracts

Emergency contacts

Employment history
This becomes the “source of truth” for HR.

✅ 2. Payroll Integration or Payroll Support

Even if some systems don’t natively run payroll in every country, they connect to payroll or provide:

Salary data

Timesheets

Deductions

Tax forms

✅ 3. Time & Attendance (or integrations)

Most support:

Clock-in/out

Leave schedules

Working hours syncing

Overtime calculations

If not built-in, they integrate with time-tracking systems.

✅ 4. Recruitment / Applicant Tracking (ATS)

They all provide (or integrate with):

Job posting pipelines

Applicant tracking

Interview stages

Hiring workflows

Offer letters

✅ 5. Onboarding Workflows

All platforms automate onboarding tasks, such as:

New hire accounts

Document signing

Orientation tasks

Asset hand-off

Training schedule

✅ 6. Performance Management

Common features:

Goal setting (OKRs)

Feedback cycles

360-reviews

Appraisals

Performance logs

✅ 7. Employee Self-Service Portals

Employees can:

Edit personal info

Download payslips

Request leave

Update bank info

View policies

This drastically reduces HR workload.

✅ 8. Document Management

All include document storage for:

Contracts

HR policies

Certificates

ID scans

Performance records

Often with electronic signatures.

✅ 9. Compliance & Auditing

They help companies follow:

Labor regulations

Tax requirements

Data privacy

Audit trails

Especially important in corporate settings.

✅ 10. Reporting & Analytics

All systems provide dashboards on:

Headcount

Attrition (turnover)

Diversity metrics

Salary breakdown

Performance data

Data is crucial for strategic HR decisions.

✅ 11. Role-Based Permissions

They all restrict visibility:

HR sees everything

Managers see teams

Employees see only themselves

Protects privacy.

✅ 12. Integration Capabilities

Common integrations include:

Payroll software

Applicant tracking

Learning systems

Accounting/ERP

Benefits providers

Identity management (IT)

This makes the HRIS part of the wider digital ecosystem.

✅ 13. Cloud-Based Access

Almost all modern HR systems:

Run in the cloud

Use subscription licensing (SaaS)

Update automatically

Have mobile apps

✅ 14. Automation

They automate repetitive HR tasks:

Leave approvals

Onboarding sequences

Reminders

Document expiry alerts

Payroll syncs

Reduces manual work and errors.

✅ 15. Security & Privacy

All provide:

Encrypted storage

Role permissions

Compliance (GDPR, SOC2, ISO27001)

Multi-factor authentication

Employee privacy is legally sensitive.

✅ 16. Lifecycle Management

A foundational principle:
Hire → Onboard → Manage → Develop → Pay → Offboard

All HR systems support the entire employee journey.
