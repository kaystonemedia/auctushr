# Privacy Policy for KaystoneHR Attendance Extension

**Last Updated:** [Current Date]

## Overview
KaystoneHR Attendance Extension is designed to help employees track their attendance and productivity within the KaystoneHR system. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect
- **Attendance Data**: Clock in/out times, break durations, work session data, and idle time tracking
- **Location Data**: Continuous high-accuracy geolocation information for attendance verification and movement tracking
- **Web Activity Data**: 
  - All websites visited and time spent on each site
  - Clicks, scrolls, keyboard inputs, and mouse movements on all websites
  - Text content of clicked elements and form interactions
  - Page titles, URLs, and navigation patterns
- **System Activity**: 
  - Browser tabs and focus changes
  - Application usage patterns
  - Idle detection and activity monitoring
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system, extension version, and device capabilities
- **Authentication Data**: Encrypted user credentials and session tokens

## How We Use Your Information
- **Attendance Tracking**: To accurately record your work hours and breaks
- **Productivity Analysis**: To provide insights into work patterns and productivity
- **Compliance**: To meet company attendance and time tracking requirements
- **Security**: To prevent unauthorized access and ensure data integrity

## Data Storage and Security
- All data is stored securely in Firebase with encryption
- Data is synchronized with the KaystoneHR system
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
- We implement industry-standard security measures

## Data Sharing
- Data is shared only with authorized HR personnel and managers
- No data is sold or shared with third parties
- Data access is logged and monitored

## Your Rights and Controls
- **Access**: View all data collected about you
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal data
- **Granular Control**: Enable/disable specific tracking features:
  - Website activity tracking
  - Location tracking
  - Keyboard and mouse monitoring
  - Productivity analytics
- **Data Export**: Download your data in standard formats
- **Consent Withdrawal**: Revoke consent for any data collection at any time
- **Contact**: Reach out for privacy-related concerns

## Data Collection Consent
This extension requires explicit consent for:
- Tracking activity across all websites
- Continuous location monitoring
- Keystroke and input monitoring
- Productivity and behavior analysis

You can modify these permissions at any time through the extension settings.
